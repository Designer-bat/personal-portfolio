## 01 · Overview

**Novagrow** is an agricultural technology brand focused on empowering modern farmers with smarter, more sustainable growing practices.

The project delivered a complete identity system: logo suite, custom typography pairing, a nature-inspired color system, icon language, and practical brand guidelines for consistent application across every touchpoint.

## 02 · Problem

Entering a crowded agtech space, Novagrow needed a brand that feels **grounded, trustworthy, and forward-looking** — and one visual language that works equally well on packaging, field equipment, and digital products.

The identity had to scale across very different physical and digital contexts while staying recognizable, approachable, and accessible to farmers and partners alike.

## 03 · Concept

The identity leans on **clarity and restraint** — no unnecessary decoration. Strong hierarchy, generous whitespace, and a disciplined palette carry the brand instead of visual noise.

The growth-green accent provides the energy, while neutral farmland tones keep the system approachable for farmers and partners alike.

## 04 · Design Process

The system was built as a set of cohesive parts:

- **Logo System** — a primary mark plus supporting lockups designed to stay legible at small sizes and on varied backgrounds.
- **Color System** — an earthy yet vibrant palette anchored in farmland tones with a fresh growth-green accent.
- **Typography** — a dependable, highly readable pairing for both display and body copy.
- **Brand Guidelines** — a lightweight guide covering spacing, color usage, typography, and do / don't examples.

Each element was tested against the contexts where the brand actually appears — from a small mark on equipment to full-bleed packaging panels — so the system holds up everywhere.

## 05 · Final Design

A complete visual identity delivering:

- A primary logo mark plus supporting lockups
- An earthy, growth-inspired color system
- A readable display and body type pairing
- An icon language consistent with the mark
- Practical brand guidelines for spacing, color, typography, and real-world usage

## 06 · Outcome

Novagrow now has a cohesive, flexible visual identity that differentiates the startup in a crowded agtech space while remaining warm and accessible to its core audience.
