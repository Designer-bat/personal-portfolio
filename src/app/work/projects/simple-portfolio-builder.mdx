## Overview

**KrishiMitra** is a smart agriculture mobile application designed to support Nepali farmers with accessible, practical, and localized agricultural information.

The platform brings important farming tools into one place, including **AI-powered crop disease detection, agricultural expert consultation, weather information, crop tracking, and market price updates**.

The interface uses a clean green visual language inspired by agriculture and nature, while Nepali-language content makes the experience easier for local farmers to understand and use.

## Key Features

- **AI Crop Disease Detection**: Farmers can upload a photo of a crop or leaf to identify possible diseases and receive useful recommendations.
- **Agricultural Expert Consultation**: Users can connect with agricultural specialists, view their profiles and ratings, and call or chat with experts for professional guidance.
- **Smart Farming Assistant**: An interactive chatbot provides farmers with quick answers to common farming and crop-related questions.
- **Weather Information**: Displays current weather conditions, temperature, humidity, wind information, and upcoming weather forecasts to support better farming decisions.
- **Crop Management**: Farmers can add and monitor their crops, track crop progress, and receive relevant farming suggestions.
- **Market Prices**: Provides current market prices for agricultural products such as vegetables, fruits, and grains, helping farmers make better selling decisions.
- **Nepali Language Support**: The interface and information are designed around Nepali users, making agricultural technology more accessible to local farmers.
- **Simple Mobile Experience**: A clean navigation system and card-based interface make important information easy to find even for users with limited technical experience.

## Design Approach

The visual design focuses on creating a **trustworthy, simple, and agriculture-focused experience**.

A natural green color palette represents farming and growth, while soft backgrounds, rounded cards, clear icons, and large typography make the application comfortable to use on mobile devices.

The main navigation provides quick access to:

- Home
- My Crops
- Market
- Weather
- Profile
- Agricultural Assistant

The dashboard prioritizes information that farmers may need frequently, such as crop health, weather conditions, farming recommendations, and market prices.

## User Experience

The main challenge was designing a digital product that could provide several complex agricultural services without making the interface confusing.

The solution was to organize information into **simple cards, clear categories, visual indicators, and task-focused screens**.

For example, the disease detection flow allows a farmer to capture or upload a crop image and immediately see that the image is being analyzed. Similarly, weather and market information are presented using visual cards so users can understand important information quickly.

## Challenges and Learnings

One of the biggest challenges was balancing the amount of agricultural information with a simple user experience.

Farmers may need access to many different types of information, but presenting everything at once can create cognitive overload. The design therefore focuses on progressive disclosure, prioritizing the most important information first while keeping additional features easily accessible.

Another important learning was the importance of **localization**. Using Nepali language, familiar terminology, agricultural imagery, and locally relevant information helps make the product feel more useful and trustworthy for its target users.

## Technologies Used

- **Figma** — UI/UX design, wireframes, prototypes, and design system
- **React / Next.js** — Front-end development
- **CSS / Tailwind CSS** — Responsive interface styling
- **AI / Machine Learning** — Crop and disease image analysis
- **Weather API** — Weather and forecast information
- **Market Data API** — Agricultural market price information

## Outcome

KrishiMitra brings multiple essential farming services together in a single mobile experience.

The final design provides farmers with a practical way to **identify crop problems, communicate with agricultural experts, monitor weather, manage crops, and check market prices** without needing to use multiple applications.

The project demonstrates how thoughtful UI/UX design and technology can be combined to create a more accessible and useful digital agriculture experience for Nepal.
